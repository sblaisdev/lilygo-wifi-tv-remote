# Quick Getting Started Guide: TV Remote & Keyboard

Welcome! This simple guide walks you through setting up your LilyGO USB TV Remote in just a few minutes. No technical background required!

---

## What Does It Do?

You plug this little USB stick into your Smart TV (or TV box), and it turns your phone into a **remote control and keyboard**. You can navigate TV menus, adjust volume, and type search queries directly from your phone's keyboard!

```
[ Your Phone ]  ---(Wi-Fi)--->  [ USB Stick in TV ]  ---(USB)--->  [ Your TV ]
```

---

## Part 1: First-Time Setup (Connecting to Your Home Wi-Fi)

You only have to do this once!

### Step 1: Plug It In
- Plug the USB stick into any powered USB port (on your TV, computer, or a phone charger).
- The small screen on the stick will light up.

### Step 2: Look at the Screen
On the stick's screen, you will see 3 important things:
1. **WiFi**: `TV-Remote-Setup`
2. **Pass**: An 8-character password in yellow letters (e.g. `k9X2mQ8p`)
3. **Address**: `http://192.168.4.1`

*(Note: The password is randomly created every time for your security, and only appears on this screen.)*

### Step 3: Connect Your Phone to the Stick
1. On your phone, go to **Settings** > **Wi-Fi**.
2. Tap on the network named **`TV-Remote-Setup`**.
3. Type the **password shown on the USB stick's screen**, then tap Connect.

### Step 4: Open the Setup Page
1. Open your phone's web browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
2. Type this address in the top bar: **`192.168.4.1`** and tap Go.
3. A friendly setup page will appear:
   - Select your **home Wi-Fi network** from the list.
   - Type your **home Wi-Fi password**.
   - *(Optional)* You can give it a nickname, like `Living Room`.
4. Tap the green **"Save & Connect"** button.

### That's it!
The stick will save your Wi-Fi securely and restart itself. In a few seconds, the screen will turn green and show:
- **`WiFi: Connected`**
- **`http://tv-remote.local`**

---

## Part 2: How to Open and Use the Remote

Make sure your phone is back on your normal home Wi-Fi network.

### Step 1: Plug the Stick into Your TV
If you set it up using a charger or computer, unplug it and plug it directly into a **USB port on the back or side of your TV**.

### Step 2: Open the Remote Page on Your Phone
Open your phone's browser and go to:
👉 **`http://tv-remote.local`**

*(Tip: If that address doesn't load, look at the USB stick's screen for its number address, like `http://192.168.1.50`, and type that in instead.)*

The remote control will open on your phone!

---

## Part 3: Using the Remote

Here is what the buttons do:

- **Arrow Buttons (▲ ▼ ◄ ►)**: Move up, down, left, and right on your TV menus.
- **OK**: Select or click what is highlighted on the TV screen.
- **Back**: Go back one screen (like the Esc key).
- **Home**: Jump straight back to your TV's main home screen.
- **Volume (+ / -)** & **Mute**: Control the sound.
- **Play / Pause**: Start or pause videos.

### Typing on Your TV (The Best Feature!)
Instead of hunting for letters on your TV screen with arrow keys:
1. Tap the box that says **"Type search or text..."** on your phone.
2. Your phone's keyboard will pop up.
3. Type any movie title, password, or search term just like sending a text message!
4. Tap **Send** (or Enter) and it types instantly onto your TV screen.

---

## Part 4: Add It to Your Phone's Home Screen (Optional)

You can save the remote as an icon on your phone just like a regular app:

### On iPhone (Safari):
1. With the remote open, tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow at the bottom).
2. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
3. Tap **Add**. You will now have a neat remote icon on your home screen!

### On Android (Chrome):
1. With the remote open, tap the **three dots (⋮)** in the top-right corner.
2. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
3. Tap **Install**.

## Helpful Tips

- **The screen on the stick went dark. Is it broken?**  
  No! It automatically turns off its screen after 15 seconds so the light doesn't bother you while watching TV in the dark. Just **press the button on the stick once** to wake the screen back up.
- **How do I connect to a different Wi-Fi network?**  
  **Press and hold the button on the stick for 10 seconds** until the screen turns red. Release the button, then press it one more time. The stick will reset back to setup mode (`TV-Remote-Setup`) so you can enter new Wi-Fi details.
